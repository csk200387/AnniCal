November 26 to now be celebrated as Constitution Day
Bar & Bench
Published on:
20 Nov 2015, 6:11 am
1 min read
Copied
Follow Us
The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has published a gazette notification declaring November 26 as Constitution Day. Until this year, the same day has been celebrated as Law Day, to commemorate the adoption of the Constitution of India by the Constituent Assembly.
