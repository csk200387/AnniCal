Back | Print
Government of India has decided to Observe 26 November as "Constitution Day' " each year. This was the day when the Constitution of India was adopted in 1949. Please refer to Gazette Notification No. F.No. 19022/11/2015.VI (E) dated November 19, 2015 notified by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, Government of India.
