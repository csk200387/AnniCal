Where There’s a Need, There’s a Knight 
 Charity is our first principle, guiding our mission for more than 140 years. Every year, the numbers speak for themselves.*
 2.2M Members
 With more than 2 million members in 17,000 councils, we have brothers around the world serving as Christ’s hands and feet to the vulnerable in their communities.
 $200M+ Donated
 We stand by our commitment to protect those in need and uphold the dignity of every human life. A portion of our earnings is used to fund our charitable work.
 48M Volunteer Hours
 Knights generously volunteer their time, impacting countless lives as they serve their parishes and communities.
