                           NOTE BY THE DIRECTOR-GENERAL

     DAY OF REMEMBRANCE FOR ALL VICTIMS OF CHEMICAL WARFARE

1.       The Conference of the States Parties (the Conference) at its Twentieth Session decided
         that the Day of Remembrance for All Victims of Chemical Warfare would be observed
         in accordance with its decision C-20/DEC.10 (dated 3 December 2015).

2.       The Chairperson of the Conference will preside over the ceremony. The day will be
         observed with a moment of silence during the Thirtieth Session of the Conference on
         26 November 2025 in the King Willem-Alexander Theater of the World Forum.

3.       This annual observance serves as a poignant reminder of the tragic human cost
         associated with chemical weapons. It is not only a solemn tribute to the countless lives
         lost and affected by these inhumane weapons, but also a powerful call to the
         international community to reinforce its commitment to the Chemical Weapons
         Convention. By commemorating this day, we honour the resilience and courage of
         survivors and reiterate our collective resolve to prevent the recurrence of such atrocities.
         The Technical Secretariat has developed a social media kit for stakeholders to raise the
         visibility of the Day of Remembrance. The kit contains ready‑made graphics and
         suggested social media messages that can be used on stakeholders’ own social media
         channels. This resource is provided in all official languages of the OPCW
         (https://www.opcw.org/remembrance).

4.       Additional information may be obtained from Ms Yvonne Apea Mensah, Head of the
         Political Affairs and Protocol Branch, who can be reached by email (protocol@opcw.org).

                                      PROGRAMME

Date and time:

Wednesday, 26 November 2025, from 9:45 to 10:00 (prior to the proceedings of the third day
of the Thirtieth Session of the Conference).

Venue:

King Willem‑Alexander Theater, World Forum.

Procedure:

At 9:45, the Chairperson of the Conference will deliver brief remarks announcing the
observance of a moment of silence.

During the moment of silence, a silent video will play on the screen of the Theater.

The end of the video will mark the close of the ceremony.

After the ceremony, the third day of the Conference session will commence.

Written statements by the Director-General of the Technical Secretariat of the OPCW, the
Mayor of the Municipality of The Hague, and the Secretary-General of the Ministry of Foreign
Affairs of the Kingdom of the Netherlands will be posted on the public website of the OPCW.
