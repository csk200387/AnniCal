National Bubble Wrap Appreciation Day
Published on January 26, 2026
National Bubble Wrap Appreciation Day (also known as National Bubble Wrap Day) is always on the last Monday in January of every year. It recognizes a unique invention that now has 400 uses. This unofficial holiday began when a radio station unwrapped microphones on the air and listeners loved it! The day is now commemorated with activities such as crafting bubble wrap outfits, organizing bubble wrap popping competitions, or throwing a party where all the furniture and utensils are bubble wrapped.
