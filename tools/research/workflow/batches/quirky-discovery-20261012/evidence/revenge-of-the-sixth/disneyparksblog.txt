Take the High Ground and Celebrate ‘Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith’
Contributed By
Nathalie Franco
Disney Consumer Products
Have you sensed a disturbance in the Force?
Greetings Disney Parks Blog fans and happy Star Wars Day! We hope you’ve saved up some energy after all the fun shopping this morning, because we’ve got even more surprises in store. You asked, and we listened, so gather up your galactic credits as Disney Store and Disney Parks are excited to reveal an all-new collection dedicated to Star Wars: Revenge of the Sith.
Revenge of the Sith celebrates its 20th anniversary this year, and Disney Store is honoring the occasion by hosting its first ever Revenge of the Sixth Day; celebrate the day on Tuesday, May 6 with commemorative items inspired by the film going on sale starting 8 a.m. PST on DisneyStore.com.
And since Sith Lords are my specialty (spoiler alert, this movie is HUGE on Sith Lords), I can confirm that this collection will be a must-have for fans and collectors alike complete with a celebratory logo and limited-edition items. As Anakin Skywalker once said, “This is where the fun begins.”
Out of this Galaxy Apparel and Accessories
Celebrate Revenge of the Sixth Day with your own Revenge of the Sith tee. Featuring artwork inspired by one of the theatrical release posters and the iconic final battle of Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi on Mustafar, you’ll always have the higher ground while wearing this tee.
Don a look fit for the Queen of Naboo herself with this gorgeous Padmé Amidala Ear Headband by BaubleBar which was inspired by Padmé’s silken nightdress (as seen in the film) and is available on DisneyStore.com. With tiny, simulated pearls and a simulated gemstone crusted tiara, this item is perfect to wear as you’re being held by your loved one while overlooking the lakes of Naboo. Talk about dreamy.
Home Goods, Toys, and Collectibles
Jedi or Sith, this collection has something for everyone. Need a new mug to enjoy your morning blue milk from Bantha? Look no further than this Revenge of the Sith commemorative mug (Bantha and blue milk not included) or add a new miniature Disney Lacers to your collection which depicts the pull from light to darkness with Anakin Skywalker on one side and Darth Vader on the other.
And for our pin traders – check out the Limited Release Revenge of the Sith pin which will have you daydreaming of speeding around Coruscant on Jedi Council missions.
Recreate one of the most fateful battles in the Star Wars saga with 10-inch Anakin Skywalker and Obi-Wan Kenobi talking action figures. This 2-pack contains both talking action figures with button activated character phrases from the battle on Mustafar, motion activated sounds, and light-up lightsabers. Certainly, a powerful pair.
Enjoy a thrill with no spills with the Revenge of the Sith CORCKCICLE Tumbler coming only to DisneyStore.com. Triple insulted and able to hold 30 ounces of liquid, the travel tumbler and tumbler with straw celebrate the 20th anniversary of the film and will be a most impressive companion throughout all your galactic journeys.
Are you intrigued by the light side, dark side, or perhaps both? This lightsaber set was made for you. Feel the pull from good to not-so-good with the Anakin Skywalker Lightsaber Set which comes with lightsaber hilt, blade, stand, and clip. The blade can also be set to blue or red to signify the pull Anakin felt while transforming from Jedi Knight to Sith Lord Darth Vader.
And that’s not all as there’s even more Star Wars Revenge of the Sith merchandise to be found on DisneyStore.com starting May 6, 2025 at 8 a.m. PST.
Can’t wait to start shopping and are local to a Disney Park? You’re in luck as this collection is available at Walt Disney World Resort and Disneyland Resort starting May 4, 2025. Lastly, for our digital shoppers who can’t get enough of the Revenge of the Sith merchandise, be sure to also check out new product reveals on the Amazon Star Wars brand store page with Revenge of the Sith product launching May 6, 2025.
No matter what side of the Force your journey leads you on, we hope you have an amazing Star Wars Day and will catch you again soon in a galaxy far, far away!
