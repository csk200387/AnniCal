From declaring independence on 26 march 1971 till the victory won on 16 December, 3 million people sacrificed their lives for the sake of independence. To pay homage and tribute to the martyrs, various programs and activities are performed throughout the country in due solemnity and festivity, Thousands of people from home and abroad show respect and affirm commitment to serve the nation on this day at National Memorial at Savar. Your visit on this day will give you an opportunity to discover the strength and valor of the people of Bangladesh How To Go Jatiyo Smriti Soudha can be reached by road from any place of the country.
