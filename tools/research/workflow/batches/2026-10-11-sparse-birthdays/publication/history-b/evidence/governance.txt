Introduction Good Governance Day is observed annually on December 25 , the birth anniversary of former Prime Minister Atal Bihari Vajpayee , whose leadership emphasised accountability, transparency, and inclusive growth. The day reminds us that governance is not just about administration, but about making life better for every citizen. To commemorate his values, the Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions publishes the Good Governance Index —a composite assessment of governance performance across states and union territories, translating policy intent into measurable, citizen-centric outcomes. India has recorded progress across sectors, driven by the implementation of multiple national-level schemes, as per the data. Man of the Masses: Atal Bihari Vajpayee Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee (1924–2018) served as Prime Minister of India on three occasions. He had a distinguished four-decade parliamentary career, was elected nine times to the Lok Sabha and twice to the Rajya Sabha. Rising from humble beginnings in Gwalior to India's highest office, he received the Padma Vibhushan in 1992 for his selfless dedication to the nation and was conferred with Bharat Ratna, India’s highest civilian honour, in 2015. Shri Atal Bihari Vajpayee championed democratic ideals, women's empowerment, and social equality. As Prime Minister, he prioritised good governance and economic development—building national highways, rural roads, expanding telecom, and improving infrastructure. His legacy continues to guide India's vision and focus on transparency, accountability, and efficient, citizen-centric service delivery. Named “Best Parliamentarian” in 1994, the citation praised him as “a multi-faceted personality” whose work consistently reflected an unwavering commitment to nationalism. Building on this vision, the Government of India measures and promotes good governance systematically across the nation. Good governance is participatory, consensus oriented, accountable, transparent, responsive, effective and efficient, equitable and inclusive, and follows the rule of law , according to the United Nations. Measuring Good Governance Good Governance Index The Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) introduced the Good Governance Index on December 25, 2019, to evaluate governance performance across states and union territories and encourage improvements . Good governance is crucial for the welfare of citizens, inclusive development and the country's economic transformation. The Index identifies 10 key sectors. Governance performance across these sectors is measured in terms of 58 indicators designed keeping citizen-centricity in mind.
