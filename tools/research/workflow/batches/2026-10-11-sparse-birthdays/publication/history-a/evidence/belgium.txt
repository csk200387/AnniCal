Disrupted services on 9 and 12 October
Due to national trade union actions, the services at the various counters of the Brucity Administrative Centre and liaison offices will be disrupted on Friday 9 and Monday 12 October 2026. Need a document or want to make an appointment? Discover the online services on External siteMyBXL. The External siteSTIB-MIVB public transport company also expects major disruptions on its network of metro, trams and buses.
